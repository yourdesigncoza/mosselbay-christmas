    Around the bay

    Mossel Bay is where the Garden Route begins. The town curves around a north-facing bay, so the main beaches are sheltered, and Santos Beach is within walking distance of the house. Bartolomeu Dias anchored here in 1488, and you can still post a letter at the tree where Portuguese sailors left theirs. December brings long, warm days: boat trips to Seal Island, sundowners at The Point, and the cliff path past the lighthouse.
